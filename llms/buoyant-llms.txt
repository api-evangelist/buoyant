# Buoyant

> Buoyant is the creator and primary maintainer of Linkerd, the CNCF graduated
> open source service mesh for Kubernetes. Buoyant provides Enterprise Linkerd
> with commercial support, security, and compliance features for organizations
> running Linkerd in production.

## Product

- [Enterprise Linkerd](https://www.buoyant.io/linkerd-enterprise): Commercial distribution of Linkerd with enterprise support, FIPS compliance, and lifecycle management
- [Pricing](https://www.buoyant.io/pricing): Plans and pricing for Enterprise Linkerd
- [Solutions Overview](https://www.buoyant.io/solutions): Use cases including zero trust security, edge Kubernetes, and FIPS compliance
- [Zero Trust Security](https://www.buoyant.io/zero-trust-in-kubernetes-with-linkerd): Zero trust network security in Kubernetes with Linkerd
- [FIPS 140-2 and 140-3](https://www.buoyant.io/fips-kubernetes-service-mesh): FIPS validated encryption in transit for Kubernetes
- [Kubernetes at the Edge](https://www.buoyant.io/kubernetes-service-mesh-edge): Service mesh for edge Kubernetes deployments
