We're happy to announce the release of Linkerd 2.18. The theme of this release is battlescars: we've added features and updated functionality to reduce operational pain in response to real life, hard-won lessons we've learned with our customers who have pushed the limits of what Linkerd (and Kubernetes itself!) can do.
