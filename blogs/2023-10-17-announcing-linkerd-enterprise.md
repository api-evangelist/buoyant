Today, we're happy to announce the release of Linkerd Enterprise, the first-ever distribution of Linkerd focused specifically on enterprise environments. Over the past 18 months, enterprise adoption of Linkerd has skyrocketed, with companies like Adidas, Microsoft, Plaid, and DB Schenker publishing case studies about how they've deployed Linkerd to bring security, compliance, and reliability to their mission-critical production infrastructure.
