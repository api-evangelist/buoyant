When it comes to observability, Linkerd already does the hard part. Every meshed pod runs a proxy that's measuring success rates, recording latencies, enforcing mTLS, and emitting a steady stream of golden metrics. By the time a request crosses a mesh boundary, your service has already produced more telemetry about its own health than most production systems generate in a week.
